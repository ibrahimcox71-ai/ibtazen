# Content, Copy & Growth

Read this when writing UI copy/brand voice, designing onboarding or retention mechanics, or
working on a pricing/checkout page.

## 1. Brand Voice & UX Copywriting

- Define voice with 2-3 adjectives + a contrast ("confident but not arrogant; plain, not
  corporate") rather than a vague mood board.
- Every button label states the outcome, not the mechanism: "Start free trial" not "Submit";
  "Save changes" not "OK".
- Error messages: say what went wrong, in human language, and what to do next — never a raw code
  or "An error occurred."
- Empty states are a copy opportunity, not just a placeholder: explain what will appear here and
  give a first action, don't just say "No items."
- Use the `design:ux-copy` skill for line-by-line microcopy (CTAs, errors, empty states,
  confirmations) once the broader flow is designed. If that skill isn't available, write the
  microcopy yourself using the rules in this section.

## 2. Onboarding & Retention Loops

- Get the user to their "first value moment" in the fewest possible steps — identify what that
  moment is explicitly (first message sent, first report generated, first item saved) and design
  onboarding backward from it.
- Don't gate the value moment behind a long signup form; delay account creation until after the
  user has seen the payoff where possible (try-before-signup).
- Use progressive onboarding (contextual tips on first use of a feature) over a single long
  tour that's forgotten immediately.
- Build at least one retention loop deliberately: a reason to come back (notification, digest,
  streak, new content) tied to real value, not just re-engagement for its own sake.
- Referral mechanics work best when the reward is immediate and the ask is a single, specific
  action ("Invite one teammate to unlock X") rather than an open-ended "invite friends."

## 3. Pricing Page Optimization

- Default to 3 tiers; anchor with the tier you want most people to pick as the visually
  emphasized middle option.
- State what's _not_ included as clearly as what is — ambiguity here causes support tickets and
  churn, not just lost signups.
- Annual vs. monthly toggle should show the actual saved amount/percentage, not just cheaper
  monthly-equivalent math buried in fine print.
- Put an FAQ addressing the top 3-5 objections (refunds, cancellation, what happens to my data)
  directly on the pricing page — objection-handling near the CTA reduces drop-off more than moving
  it to a separate page.

## 4. Checkout Optimization (e-commerce/SaaS)

- Minimize fields: don't ask for anything you don't need to complete the transaction; ship
  address autocomplete, don't force account creation before purchase (offer guest checkout).
- Show a persistent order/cart summary throughout checkout, including total cost with taxes/fees
  up front — hidden costs revealed late are the single biggest cause of cart abandonment.
- Display trust signals near the payment field specifically (security badges, accepted payment
  icons), not just somewhere on the page.
- Multi-step checkout should show progress (step 2 of 3) and allow going back without losing
  entered data.
- Confirm the order clearly post-purchase (on-screen + email) with what happens next (shipping
  timeline, access instructions) — this reduces "did it work?" support load.
